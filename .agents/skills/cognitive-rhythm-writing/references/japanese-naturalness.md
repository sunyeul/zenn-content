# Japanese Naturalness Review

Use this pass only for Japanese prose. Preserve the parent skill's content contract.

## Review Loop

1. Detect repeated or overly uniform patterns.
2. Judge each finding in its genre and local context.
3. Revise only findings that make the prose mechanical, vague, or needlessly dense.
4. Re-read the whole passage; keep intentional patterns with a reason.

## Rhythm and Structure Signals

- Similar sentence lengths across a long passage or paragraphs with mechanically identical sentence counts.
- Repeated sentence endings, openings, connectors, or contrast frames such as `〜ではなく`.
- No nominal endings in substantial prose, or nominal endings inserted so often that the prose becomes slogan-like.
- Repeated `label: explanation`, colon headings, agenda-summary templates, excessive emphasis, or uniformly exhaustive lists.

Repair the smallest useful unit. Mix sentence lengths by merging as well as splitting; do not turn every sentence short. Preserve lists, headings, and repetition when they improve lookup, comparison, or deliberate emphasis.

## Diction and Agency Signals

- Stock phrases such as `〜についてご紹介します`, repeated `〜を活用する`, or verbose `〜することができます` where direct wording is clearer.
- Abstract movement verbs that obscure the actual action.
- Passive constructions or omitted actors that make responsibility unclear.
- Transitions such as `さらに`, `加えて`, `また`, and `一方で` repeated without doing real logical work.

Prefer concrete verbs and name the actor when it matters. For a casual tone, connect actions to what the reader can try or learn rather than sprinkling `〜ですよね` or `試してみましょう` through every section. Apply the voice to result interpretations and caveats as well as the introduction. Keep conventional technical phrasing when replacing it would reduce precision.

## Voice and Evidence

Use verified experience, failed attempts, judgment, and limitation when the source material contains them. Never invent personal experience, emotion, opinion, or failure to make AI-generated prose sound human.

## Guardrails

- Findings are suspicions, not errors. Optimize for truthful, readable prose rather than zero findings.
- Do not copy corpus thresholds without the corresponding lint tool, corpus, and genre calibration.
- Do not mechanically add questions, casual endings, nominal endings, or sentence-opening repetition.
- Do not rewrite quotations, code, citations, numbers, or established terminology for surface variety.
